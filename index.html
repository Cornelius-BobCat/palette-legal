Assistance

Palette est un casse-tête de logique : peins chaque case en rouge, jaune ou bleu, en t'aidant des taches qui montrent le mélange des couleurs voisines. Deux cases voisines n'ont jamais la même couleur.

Support

Palette is a logic puzzle: paint each cell red, yellow or blue, guided by blots that show the mix of the neighboring colors. Two touching cells never share a color.

A question or a problem?

Email us at corneliusvincentgit@gmail.com with your device model and what happened (a screenshot helps a lot).

FAQ

My progress is gone. It is stored only on the device: it is lost if the app is uninstalled.

I want to change my ad choices. In the game: Settings › Ad privacy.

I bought "Remove ads" on another iPhone. In the game: Settings › Restore purchases.
